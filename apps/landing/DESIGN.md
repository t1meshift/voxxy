# voxxy landing

A dark pine first screen with warm paper for the chat, a peach send button and an asymmetric sound wave as the brand mark. The pine outline keeps the wave visible on light surfaces and at favicon size. Bricolage Grotesque sets the headings and DM Sans the controls and body. Both fonts are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.

The headline and live chat share the desktop viewport. On narrow screens the chat follows the short introduction in reading order. The rest of the page switches to paper and follows the audio-in, application, audio-out path before the code example.

The chat starts empty. Use status text for downloads, recording and reply generation. Model names stay in the test stand and in the default provider wiring.
